Why This Program

  • Plyometrics raise jump height. 70-study meta: CMJ SMD 0.69; RSI 0.80; SLJ 1.34. Scientific Reports 2025
  • Descending Complex Training — all strength sets before plyometrics. Dietz & Peterson 2012
  • PAPE rest window: 3 min after strength block. Dietz & Peterson 2012
  • Annual periodization — 4 phases across 52 weeks, deloads every 4th week. Issurin 2010
  • In-season maintenance: 2 sessions/week preserves strength and power. Mujika & Padilla 2003
  • Loaded strength correlates with jump (Wisløff 2004: 1RM half-squat r=0.78). This program is bodyweight-based — it builds the pattern and tendon resilience, not the loaded strength that correlation describes.
  • Unilateral work reduces asymmetry. SMD 0.71. Sun 2025
  • Dynamic warm-ups beat static. Static: SMD −0.17. Warneke 2024
